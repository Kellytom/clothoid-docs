# Work items, plates and the board

The project keeps three registers, and they are easy to confuse.

- **P** numbers are **plates** on the ornament board: 85 of them. P01–P20 are the
  fleur studies; P21–P65 catalogue ornaments; P66–P92 class I caps; P93–P101
  class II leaves; P102–P105 presets.
- **W** numbers are **work items**, W01–W26: what has to be built.
- **D** numbers are **decisions**, D01–D11: choices already made, with reasons.

The board is `docs/ornament-board.html`, generated by
`scripts/board/build-board.mjs`; the fleur studies are its subpage. `BACKLOG.md`
holds the detail behind each W item.

## The order

1. **W01** piece model — `Piece`, `Trace`, multi-trace render. Done.
2. **W02** terminals — caps and tapers; the 120° rule defines a cap. Done,
   turned terminals are not.
3. **W03** fleur grammar — study 01 ported; `fleur_canonical()` is the next
   action.
4. **W04** the twenty parameter sets.
5. **Primals** — earth, water, air, fire. Fire needs the turned terminals.
6. **Archetypes** — fleur, acanthus, palmette.

## The spine layer's gate

The spine layer is central to the curve work but not yet part of the tool. Four
conditions promote it, and all four must hold:

1. `clothoid_lab` imports a chain and builds a `Piece`/`Trace` from its anchors.
2. One spine renders on the 200 × 250 mm sheet, +y up, through `svg.document`.
3. `piece_clearance()` measures it, and a declared contact passes.
4. One W03 study is drawn entirely from Pythagorean chains.

Until then, "central" is intent. The gate is repeated in `PLAN` and
`ARCHITECTURE` on purpose: it is the one thing that decides the order of work.
